## ED1 — attention and the patchwork

- **a.** Unit: a six-hour block between 15 and 23 June 2026. Kept: blocks with ≥ 30 task edits (18 blocks; 3,680 of 3,799 task edits of the period). Statistic: share of the block's task edits on its five busiest pages; reference: min(5, pages)/pages. No block has fewer than five pages. Unchanged from the previous ED1a.
- **b.** Unit: a pair of uses of a convention (a use = an edit with strictly more of one form). Same-page and different-page agreement as before. **New**: 1,000 permutations (was the median of 50) of the forms within 3-h blocks, and 1,000 within task family of the page × 3-h block; 95% bootstrap over pages (1,000). Result: above the 97.5th percentile for 15/16 (time) and 12/16 (task × time); median excess 0.084 and 0.024. Exceptions: termination/teardown (both), deadline/horizon, signal/ping, task-clock (task × time). 69–89% of uses are in a family-block group with ≥ 2 pages.
